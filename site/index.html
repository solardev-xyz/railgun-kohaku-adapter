01 / Why we built it

A protocol is only part
of a working wallet.

A privacy integration has to survive everything around the proof: account state, user reviews, process exits, uncertain delivery and changing circuits.

We built this while integrating Railgun into Freedom. The work became a separate package so another wallet can adopt the same account and operation machinery without inheriting Freedom’s source tree.

Railgun supplies the protocol and cryptography. Kohaku supplies the interface vocabulary. This adapter connects them to the capabilities a wallet actually owns.

01

Keep custody with the wallet

The host owns the vault, signing, storage and network policy. The adapter works through limited credential loans and explicit reviews.

02

Make interruption a normal state

Preserve proved operations and durable attempts. Authenticate the original hold on reopen. Unknown delivery leads to observation, never an automatic resend.

03

Give adopters a real example

An installed headless Electron application with separate wallets, encrypted stores, reproducible setup and a guided end-to-end flow.

02 / End to end

Alice pays Bob.
Bob owns the next move.

Two independent seeds, stores, funding EOAs and Railgun identities. Bob discovers the payment through his own scan; Alice’s database never becomes his wallet.

Step 01 · public → private

◇ → ◆

Start with Alice’s own custody.

A fresh profile holds Alice’s vault and enrolled public funding account. The public Kohaku adapter prepares the Shield; its submitter handles the reviewed operation over genuine owner capabilities.

After settlement, Alice scans and refreshes her wallet to find her received private note.

BoundaryShielding and gas funding are public-chain activity. Direct submission does not demonstrate relay privacy.

Step 02 · foreign private transfer

A → ◆ → B

A real recipient, an authenticated operation.

Alice supplies Bob’s Railgun address. The private adapter wraps a genuine owner lane, which checks the selected note, binds the destination, proves the operation and retains it before submission.

Reviews remain distinct from guide navigation. A timeout or expired review does not authorize a new payment.

RecoveryA proved, unjournaled hold can use supported cold submission. Unknown delivery is observed rather than resent.

Step 03 · proof of innocence

◆ + ✓

Prove the output’s list eligibility.

Alice synchronizes TXID state and prepares the transaction’s output POI using authenticated membership, roots and pinned circuit artifacts. A separate review covers the disclosure to the configured service.

The handoff is durably recorded. A response category is diagnostic; it does not establish that Bob’s output is accepted.

Live evidenceOne handoff occurred in the frozen run. Its response was classified malformed; Bob’s later owned Valid status establishes acceptance for his output.

Step 04 · recipient ownership

B ↻ ◆

Bob discovers it himself.

Bob’s fresh process scans using his own vault and store. His wallet finds the exact payment note, synchronizes its TXID state and observes POI Valid for that owned Transact note.

The guide exchanges payment-identifying information, not seeds or databases. Keep that exchange private: transaction identifiers can link activity.

ScopeThe live run used independent Alice/Bob custody. An unrelated third-account ownership control has synthetic coverage only.

Step 05 · private → public

◆ → ◇

Bob signs with Bob’s authority.

Bob selects his owned, unspent, POI-Valid note. Preparation and signing repeat the owner checks before unshielding to his enrolled public account under the configured amount and gas limits.

In the frozen live run, an expired review left a genuine proved hold. Supported cold recovery submitted that same hold with a fresh review and the original private spending signature.

One transactionNo replacement unshield proof, duplicated payment or mid-run package change was needed for that recovery.

Step 06 · observed completion

▤ = ✓

Check what actually happened.

The run checked all three successful receipts against recorded inclusion identities and finality, plus configured gas ceilings, Shield net value, transfer value and unshield received value plus Railgun fee.

Both available-note lists were empty after their respective final refreshes. Separate actor nonce checks provided an additional RPC cross-check.

TrustObservations retain unverified-rpc. Available-note residual checks are not a full EOA balance reconciliation or an independent light-client proof.
About automation

The saved guide chooses steps with ordinary code and calls the original reviews. No LLM is part of those rules. An AI agent operated the completed live run; a separate human usability walkthrough remains to be done.

03 / Architecture

A clear division
of responsibility.

No new privacy protocol. No application master seed exported. The package authenticates supplied runtime archives and artifacts; host callbacks remain trusted capabilities.

01 / YOUR APPLICATION

The host

  • UI or command-line reviews
  • Vault and credential loans
  • Context, profile and storage
  • Processes and observed closure
  • RPC / Tor transport policy
  • EOA signer and transaction journal
Authority stays in the adopting wallet

02 / THIS PACKAGE

The adapter + owners

  • Restricted Kohaku interfaces
  • Account enrollment and scan state
  • Owned notes and operation lanes
  • Preparation and proving jobs
  • Retained custody and recovery
  • POI / TXID coordination
Fixed purposes, explicit lifetimes

03 / UPSTREAM

Railgun + services

  • Railgun engine algorithms
  • Prover and circuit artifacts
  • Deployed contracts
  • Public chain RPC
  • TXID indexer and POI service
  • Configured broadcaster transport
Pinned inputs, observed deployment

06 / What the work taught us

The hard parts were
around the happy path.

↻

A locally valid proof can be obsolete.

The historical campaign’s POI circuit had rotated. A proof accepted by the retired key was rejected by the current key. An explicit replacement transition and current-artifact verification restored the path.

Artifact update discipline ↗
⌁

Availability is part of wallet design.

Large scans, slow services, Tor failures and expiring reviews exposed gaps that fast fixtures missed. Returned progress, bounded recovery and remaining review time now have explicit treatment.

Delayed-service qualification ↗
▤

Custody is not a disposable cache.

Derived scan state can be rebuilt under compatible policies. Signatures, retained operations and submission attempts keep their authenticated history through restart and reviewed upgrades.
